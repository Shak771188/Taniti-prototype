Travel & Transportation

You can reach our island by air. Our small airport welcomes small jets and propellers. If arriving by cruise, we offer docking once a week at Yellow Leaf Bay. Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Taxis are available in Taniti City, and rental cars can be rented from a local rental agency near the airport. Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors (helmets are required by law).

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the power options

Power outlets are 120 volts (the same as in the United States).

Island Alcohol Laws

Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m. The drinking age on Taniti is 18 and the drinking age is not strictly enforced.

What is the primary language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by the older residents.
